## 8. Method limits

- Near-duplicate detection is a heuristic on 48x48 greyscale thumbnails. Heavily cropped, rescaled or recoloured copies can be missed, so duplicate counts are minimums.
- Single-linkage grouping can chain together different photographs that look alike; the sensitivity table shows how much the counts move with the threshold.
- Smooth, low-texture images (for example close-up leaf blades) give weak structure for the comparison, so duplicates among them may be under-detected.
- The script cannot judge whether a label is biologically correct. Flagged images need human review.
